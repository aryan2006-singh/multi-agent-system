## Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original
payment method. To request a refund, go to Account > Billing >
Request Refund, or contact support with your invoice number.

## Subscription Plans

We offer Basic (₹499/month), Pro (₹999/month), and Enterprise
(custom pricing). You can upgrade or downgrade anytime from
Account > Subscription. Downgrades take effect at the next billing
cycle; upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed automatically after every successful payment
and are also available under Account > Billing > Invoice History as
downloadable PDFs.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, we retry automatically 3 times over 5 days.
After 3 failed attempts, the subscription is paused until payment
details are updated.
