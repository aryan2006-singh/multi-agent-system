## Contact Information

Support is available via chat 24/7, email at support@example.com,
and phone during business hours (9 AM - 6 PM, Mon-Fri).

## Company Policies

We follow a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. Data is
retained for 90 days after account cancellation before permanent
deletion.

## Account Management

You can update your email, password, and notification preferences
under Account > Settings. Deleting your account is permanent and
can be done from Account > Settings > Delete Account.
