## Features by Plan

Basic includes core features with a 3-project limit. Pro includes
unlimited projects, priority support, and advanced analytics.
Enterprise adds SSO, dedicated support, and custom integrations.

## Availability

The product is available globally. Some advanced analytics features
are currently limited to Pro and Enterprise plans only.

## Comparisons

Compared to competitors, we offer a more generous free tier and no
per-seat pricing on the Pro plan — pricing is per workspace, not per
user.
