## Password Reset

Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". A reset link is
sent to your registered email and is valid for 30 minutes.

## Login Issues

If login fails repeatedly, clear your browser cache and cookies, or
try an incognito window. Accounts are temporarily locked for 15
minutes after 5 failed login attempts, for security.

## Installation

The app is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Download the
installer from the official downloads page and run it with
administrator privileges. Minimum requirements: 4GB RAM, 500MB free
disk space.

## Common Errors

Error 401 means your session has expired — log in again. Error 500
is a server-side issue; if it persists for more than a few minutes,
check the status page or contact support.
